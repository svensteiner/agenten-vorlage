# Research brief

Identify the decision and constraints before gathering sources. Prefer maintained primary
documentation for technical facts. Check dates for changing information. Compare suitable
options against the same criteria. Attach source URLs or local paths to material claims;
label your own inference. Report inaccessible evidence rather than implying it was read.
Deliver the recommendation, tradeoff, evidence and the next practical validation step.
