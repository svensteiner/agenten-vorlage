# Customer response

Use the provided conversation and product facts to identify the customer's actual need.
Separate confirmed details from assumptions. For sales, state fit and disqualifiers; for
support, diagnose the symptom and avoid already failed advice. Draft a concise response
with one useful next action. Record unresolved authority or product questions separately.
Do not send, issue refunds or promise discounts without explicit authorization.
